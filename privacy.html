Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

"Kirchhoff Valley n8n" is a private automation used by the owner of the Kirchhoff Valley channel to publish the owner's own videos. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What the tool accesses

With the channel owner's permission, the tool connects to the owner's own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API to upload videos and set their titles, descriptions, tags and subtitles. It reads and writes only the content of that one channel.

What is collected and stored

The tool does not collect personal data from viewers or any other third party. The only credential it stores is the owner's own OAuth token, kept inside the owner's private n8n installation. Nothing is sent to any other service.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared or disclosed to anyone.

YouTube and Google

The tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it the owner is bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated.
